# Editorial spot-colour illustrations

These illustrations give a tool or an article a visual register without
explaining it. A diagram carries a claim; an illustration only supports the
subject already named by the adjacent heading and summary. If position,
connection or quantity in the image has to be understood, draw a diagram
instead.

## The fixed language

- **Format:** 1200 × 750 PNG. One file is used unchanged in light and dark
  mode; there is no mode pair.
- **Composition:** one person, object or still-life gesture, drawn as five to
  eight large silhouettes in a flattened, almost orthographic view. Leave
  generous, crop-safe space around it.
- **Colour:** quiet, nearly neutral warm-white ground, near-black charcoal, warm greys and
  taupes, plus exactly one small `#FF8700` detail. No second accent.
- **Form:** broad, hard-edged matte planes with deliberately simplified
  proportions. A sparse dark line may describe a cable, edge or joint, but
  never a relationship or process.
- **Texture:** one regular halftone field in one contained plane. It is the
  print texture of the illustration, not the dashed-state vocabulary of a
  diagram.
- **Depth:** use overlap and at most two flat tones per object. One pale,
  shape-like ground wash is allowed; realistic cast shadows, highlights,
  materials and continuous modelling are not. The wash does not license a
  CSS shadow, gradient or elevation anywhere in the interface.
- **People:** adult proportions, quiet posture, no identifiable facial detail.
- **Accessibility:** the files are decorative support. Use an empty `alt`
  when the adjacent copy already names the subject.

## Prompt

Replace `[SUBJECT]` and leave the rest unchanged:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: decorative teaser illustration, wide 8:5 landscape

Primary request: Create a mature editorial spot-colour illustration of
[SUBJECT] for a restrained technical design system.

Style/medium: flat drawn editorial illustration with a restrained
cut-paper/printmaking character; broad hard-edged matte silhouettes;
deliberately simplified geometry; at most two flat tones per object; one
controlled field of regular vintage halftone dots.

Composition/framing: 1200 × 750; one clear subject or still-life gesture;
flattened, almost orthographic view with very shallow perspective; generous
nearly neutral warm-white negative space matching #F7F6F2; crop-safe on all
sides; readable at teaser-card size; no border or frame.

Colour palette: warm off-white, near-black charcoal, warm grey, muted taupe,
and exactly one small vivid orange #FF8700 element. No other hue.

Rendering rules: five to eight dominant silhouettes; clean simplified
contours; no small functional detail. A sparse line may describe a cable,
edge or joint but must not form a process or diagram. Put the halftone inside
one contained object plane only. If grounding is needed, use one pale flat
wash shaped like a shadow, never realistic lighting.

Constraints: decorative, not explanatory; no text, letters, numbers, logos,
watermark, labels, arrows, charts, workflows, code or UI screens.

Avoid: photorealism, product rendering, realistic perspective, realistic
materials, lenses, reflections, highlights, continuous shading, cast-shadow
gradients, paper grain, cream or parchment backgrounds, bright multicolour
palettes, dramatic lighting, glossy 3D, childish character design, retro
travel posters, speech bubbles, dense detail, fine hatching and decorative
clutter.
```
