What is shared is the construction, not the drawing
Every mark is a square 96 × 96 viewBox holding a 96 × 72 construction, outer corners at radius 12, rounding half the stroke, gap never less than the stroke, and orange used exactly once — in the top-right corner. Everything else belongs to the product. Each ships three files, drawn on the pixel grid of the size it is for and never scaled between them.
